\textbf{\textcolor{accent}{1. Executive Summary}} ---
Default tutorial training recipes severely underfit multi-class vision tasks, misdiagnosing minor classes and confusing visually similar categories. Across two containerized PyTorch applications, systematic Hyperparameter Optimization (HPO) lifted untouched test-set accuracy by \textbf{+30.33 pp} on FashionMNIST (\texttt{QuickstartMLP} 64.28\% $\rightarrow$ \texttt{GarmentConvNet} \textbf{94.61\%}) and \textbf{+36.69 pp} on CIFAR-10 (\texttt{TutorialCifarNet} 55.80\% $\rightarrow$ \texttt{CifarResNet9} \textbf{92.49\%}). Optimizer and schedule tuning alone accounted for +25.68 pp on the fixed \texttt{QuickstartMLP} architecture and +11.03 pp on the fixed 62k-param \texttt{TutorialCifarNet} before architectural inductive bias, BatchNorm/skip connections, and targeted data augmentations closed the remaining accuracy gap.

\vspace{3pt}
Without HPO, overall accuracy masked catastrophic class collapse. On FashionMNIST, \texttt{QuickstartMLP} predicted only 11/1,000 Sandals correctly (524 as Sneaker, 440 as Ankle boot) and 68/1,000 Shirts (315 Coat, 248 Pullover, 246 T-shirt/top); tuned \texttt{GarmentConvNet} recovered 987 Sandals and 823 Shirts. On CIFAR-10, \texttt{TutorialCifarNet} predicted only 268/1,000 Cats (221 as Dog, 145 as Frog), 347/1,000 Deer (167 as Horse, 150 as Frog), and misclassified 230/1,000 Trucks as Automobiles; \texttt{CifarResNet9} cut \texttt{truck$\rightarrow$car} confusion to 30 and lifted Cat and Deer to 83.4\% and 93.2\%.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{\textcolor{accent}{5. Key Findings on Why HPO Is Essential Across Model Families}}
\vspace{1pt}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}\setlength{\topsep}{2pt}\setlength{\leftmargin}{14pt}
\item \textbf{Learning Rate, Optimizer \& Schedule Govern Underfitting:} Default \texttt{SGD (lr=0.001)} moves parameters too slowly in 2--5 epochs, leaving class boundaries unseparated even on simple MLPs. Tuning LR (0.01), adding momentum (0.9) or \texttt{AdamW} with \texttt{CosineAnnealingLR}, and normalizing batch size yielded +25.68 pp on FashionMNIST MLP and +11.03 pp on CIFAR-10 without changing a single layer.
\item \textbf{Capacity Requires Spatial Inductive Bias + Normalization:} Expanding plain MLP epochs plateaus near 90\%, whereas local 3$\times$3 convolutions, \texttt{BatchNorm2d}, \texttt{GELU}, and residual skip paths break through both texture ambiguity (Shirt vs.\ Coat) and RGB pose variance (CIFAR-10 animals: +41.8 to +58.5 pp).
\item \textbf{Regularization + Augmentation Govern High-Capacity Generalization:} Scaling to 1.87M--3.70M parameters alongside \texttt{Flip+Affine}, \texttt{RandomCrop+Cutout}, weight decay ($5\times 10^{-4}$), dropout (0.20--0.25), and label smoothing (0.05) suppressed train-to-test overfitting: validation vs.\ test gaps remained small (0.42 pp on FashionMNIST, 0.63 pp on CIFAR-10).
\end{itemize}
