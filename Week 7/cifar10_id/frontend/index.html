HYPERPARAMETER OPTIMIZATION STUDY

From baseline to optimized.

A quantitative comparison of the PyTorch CIFAR-10 tutorial ConvNet and our HPO-tuned ResNet-9 across the 10,000-image CIFAR-10 held-out test split.

01 / TUTORIAL BASELINE

2-Epoch Net

Following the official PyTorch CIFAR-10 tutorial, a 2-conv + 3-linear Net trains with SGD (lr=0.001, momentum=0.9, batch=4) for 2 epochs. Shallow depth and short training leave complex animal textures ambiguous.

02 / HPO SWEEP

Systematic Search

We tuned optimizers (SGD+Momentum vs. AdamW), learning rates, batch sizes, cosine annealing, residual skip connections, BatchNorm, dropout, label smoothing, and Flip+Crop+Cutout on a 45k/5k validation split.

03 / OPTIMIZED RESNET-9

Residual Context

Combining BatchNorm, GELU, skip blocks, CosineAnnealingLR, and Cutout sharply separates subtle intra-group pairs such as cat vs. dog and deer vs. horse on 32×32 RGB patches.

CONTAINER ARCHITECTURE

Cog + FastAPI separation.

All PyTorch checkpoints are verified by SHA-256 checksum and loaded once inside the Cog inference container using safetensors. The web container validates uploads, strips EXIF metadata, and calls Cog over the internal Docker network.

LOCAL & DETERMINISTIC

Stay fast.
Stay verifiable.

Prediction events are logged inside the running Cog container at /tmp/cifar10-predictions.jsonl without storing user pixel bytes or filenames.
